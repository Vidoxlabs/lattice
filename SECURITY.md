# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **admin@vidoxlabs.dev** with the affected repository, reproduction steps, potential impact and any suggested mitigation. Do not include secrets or personal data. Do not publicly disclose the issue before it has been assessed.

The scope is the public Vidoxlabs repositories, including lattice and its website pipeline. The relevant repository policies are:

- [vivarium](https://github.com/Vidoxlabs/vivarium/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
- [seed](https://github.com/Vidoxlabs/seed/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
- [bloom](https://github.com/Vidoxlabs/bloom/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
- [capillary](https://github.com/Vidoxlabs/capillary/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
- [myosotis](https://github.com/Vidoxlabs/myosotis/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
- [aestivation](https://github.com/Vidoxlabs/aestivation/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
- [drosera](https://github.com/Vidoxlabs/drosera/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
- [trellis](https://github.com/Vidoxlabs/trellis/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
- [docs](https://github.com/Vidoxlabs/docs/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
- [videsign](https://github.com/Vidoxlabs/videsign/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
- [lattice](https://github.com/Vidoxlabs/lattice/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
